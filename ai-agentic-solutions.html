What is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is an autonomous system that perceives inputs, reasons through a goal, calls the right tools and APIs inside your systems, and executes actions, then evaluates the result and adjusts, under human-in-the-loop control. Unlike a chatbot that answers questions, an agent gets things done.
